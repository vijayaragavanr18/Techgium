What this does
Each row is a shot from the manifest. The controller:
  1. Reads the device-specific VMAF floor (phone=68, tv=76; data-saver lowers each).
  2. Scales that floor down when sustained throughput is below 2.5 Mbps (adaptive to the link, so we don't stall on BSNL).
  3. Picks the rendition with the fewest bytes that meets the floor.
Try the Indian-network presets: Jio 4G metro vs BSNL 3G rural — you'll see the floor drop and renditions shrink dramatically on BSNL while meeting a higher target on Jio. this is the whole point
